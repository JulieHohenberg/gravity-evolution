How do robots learn to walk?

Genetic Evolution Algorithm: A population of robots are born into a world and must learn to walk. Only the most fit robots will survive to the next generation. Fueled by natural selection, generations of robots try to walk. Surviving robots reproduce, passing along their learnings to their children. This cycle continues, with some random mutation, for thirty generations.

Reinforcement Learning PPO Algorithm: One intelligent robot is born into a world and teaches itself how to walk.

The robots race under Earth gravity (9.81 m/s²) or Moon gravity (1.62 m/s²). Each robot senses its body angle, movement, joint positions, and the ground, but it is not told the gravity setting directly. Its controller tells four motors which way to turn and how much torque to allow at each joint.
